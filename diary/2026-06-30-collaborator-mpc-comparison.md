# Our `vlm_mpc/` vs the collaborator's `sim_free_mpc/` — same core, different output

**2026-06-30** — A collaborator shared a design doc ("Sim-Free MPC: Mathematical Logic") for a parallel
implementation, `pps/sim_free_mpc/`, integrated into `eval_steering.py --vlm_base`. This entry records
how it relates to our `vlm_mpc/`, for converging the two later. Companion to
[[2026-06-28-region-selector-steering-design]] and the GOAL.md VLM-DP/PPS thread.

**One-line relationship:** both are **DIAL-MPC sampling a geometric FK-cost** — the same idea — but ours
**executes the MPC mean directly on the robot** in Isaac (a standalone geometric controller), while
theirs **converts the MPC mean into a flow velocity that replaces the pi0 base policy** inside PPS's
velocity-space steering loop. Theirs is the GOAL.md end-state (VLM-DP as the *steerable base* for PPS);
ours is the geometric-base + perception + grasp half, validated standalone on two robots.

## Shared core (identical)
- **DIAL annealed-MPPI**: same `σ(k,h)` annealing, `softmax(−J/λ)`, weighted mean `ā`. Our `sampler.py`
  `make_accel_sampler` ↔ their `dial_sampler.py`.
- **Geometric FK cost, no sim rollout**: reach + smoothness + regularization off FK of candidate joint
  chunks. Our `costs.py` ↔ their `costs.py`.
- **Batched FK**: our `fk.py` `FrankaFK` ↔ their `fk.py` `PandaFK`.
- **FK→world frame composition**: our `WorldFK` (base SE3) ↔ their `planner.py` (`robot_root`/`panda_link0`).
- **Robotiq ee-offset calibration**: ours **0.1716** (vs FK hand) ≈ theirs **0.171574** (vs `panda_link8`) —
  *independently calibrated the same offset against the same `ee_frame`.* Strong cross-check both FK chains
  are right.

## Where they diverge (the output stage)
| | Ours (`vlm_mpc/`) | Theirs (`sim_free_mpc/`) |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | geometric base controller + grounding + grasp | VLM-DP as steerable base for PPS (end-state) |
| Decision var | joint **accelerations** → integrate | pi0 **model-space** chunk `x_t` |
| Cost goal | **camera grounding** (ReKep kp / VoxPoser affordance) | **privileged** object poses (context) |
| Cost terms | reach + orient + **yaw/straddle** + smooth | reach + terminal + smooth + delta + gripper (position-only) |
| "Denoising" | the annealed-MPPI iterations | the pi0 flow ODE (`num_steps`) |
| MPC emits | the mean trajectory | a **velocity** `v_vlm = −(ā−x_t)/|dt|` |
| Steering | none (base only) | `v_total = γ·v_vlm + α(v_task − v_ref)` |
| **→ Action** | first knot of `q_traj` → `apply_arm` (**executed**) | integrate ODE → `x_0` → `output_to_actions` (**decoded**) |
| Runs in | IsaacLab (real rollout) | pi0 inference loop |

**The action contrast (the crux):** ours — *the MPC mean **is** the action* (executed directly). Theirs —
*the MPC mean only produces a velocity; the action is the **denoised endpoint** `x_0` of the pi0 flow,
shaped by `v_vlm` + steering, then decoded.* The indirection is exactly what enables steering: because the
action is *generated by* the flow (not *equal to* the mean), the proxy residual can **bend** it. You can't
bend a directly-executed mean — which is why ours has no steering and theirs does.

Velocity vs score space: both are explicitly the **velocity/flow-space MVP** (their "strict raw score-space
sampler is deferred"; GOAL.md §4 same). Aligned on that.

## Complementarity / merge path
Not competitors — the **same design at two stages**. To take ours to the end-state: surface our internal
`(ā−x_t)` as a velocity and add the steering term — *that is theirs.* So `ours → theirs` is a small
conversion, not a rebuild. The merge: our **grounded, grasp-aware cost** (camera `p_goal` + yaw/straddle)
drops into their `J(a;c)` (currently privileged-pose + reach-only); their **`v_vlm` + steering wrapper**
closes our open PPS-residual item. One design choice to keep explicit: theirs routes through **pi0 flow
space** specifically to reuse the **existing OpenPI proxy checkpoints** (`v_task`/`v_ref`) — a pragmatic
reuse, not a fundamental requirement; a joint-space proxy could steer ours directly.

## Their "robot going crazy" — debug notes (for them; we move on)
`--vlm_base` **replaces pi0's learned base flow with `v_vlm`**, so the cost must now supply *all* the
regularization the prior used to (feasibility, orientation, in-distribution-ness). Suspects, ranked:
1. **Decode representation — RESOLVED**: their output is an absolute **joint-position** chunk
   (`a_real = (q₁…q₇, g)`), matching the task's `JointPositionAction, scale=1`. Not a delta/absolute mismatch.
2. **Joint-limit clamping**: ours clamps every candidate to `[q_lo,q_hi]` in `integrate`; theirs unspecified
   → out-of-range targets are a classic "crazy." *Check first.*
3. **Velocity/step magnitude**: `dt·v_vlm = ā−x_t` ⇒ each denoising step jumps `x` *fully* to the MPC mean,
   then adds `α(v_task−v_ref)` (sized for small normal steps) → overshoot if `α`/`γ_base`/`σ₀` aren't
   re-scaled; model-space `σ₀` vs joint normalization unspecified.
4. **Cost position-only (no orientation) + `w_reach` vs `w_delta`/`w_smooth` balance** → redundant arm
   flails to a point.
Fast isolation: print a decoded `x_0` chunk's joints (in-range/smooth?); clamp limits; set `α=0` (steering
off) — stable ⇒ steering magnitude; still crazy ⇒ base cost/limits. FK is calibrated (don't start there).
